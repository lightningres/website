2. The service

LightningRes is reservation and booking software for tour operators who run scheduled, limited-capacity trips. LightningRes is pre-launch and is currently taking early-access operators.

4. Fees and billing

  • Fees are charged per operator, metered on passengers carried each month, at the rates published on our pricing section. Prices are in Australian dollars and exclude GST, which is added to each invoice.
  • A passenger counts if their booking was confirmed at departure time, from any channel. Crew never count. Passengers on departures you cancel, and passengers who cancel before departure, do not count.
  • New operators get 90 days free. No card is needed and there is no cap.
  • We bill monthly in arrears for passengers actually carried. We tell you the amount three days before your card is charged and send you a receipt.
  • There is no annual contract and no minimum term.

5. Price changes

We change prices only on 30 days’ written notice, and new prices apply from the next billing period after the notice ends. If you cancel before then, you pay nothing further.
