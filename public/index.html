LightningRes: Agentic Resource Scheduling Engine. A deterministic, agent-native solution for tour operators.

Built for the way scheduled tours really run

LightningRes is a reservation and booking system for day boats to the reef, overnight liveaboards, day bus tours with hotel pickups and scenic flights in fixed time slots. Here is what it is built to do.

  • One inventory, every channel

    Your booking page, phone bookings with payment links, an agent portal for tour desks and wholesalers, marketplaces and distributors through one partner API (OCTO standard), and AI assistants all draw from the same seats. It is built so the last seat can never be sold twice.

  • Built for boats, buses and cabins

    Shared legs on multi-stop voyages, cabins and berths across nights, dive sessions, hotel pickups and third-party allotments live in one capacity model. Each kind of trip is modelled the way it actually runs, not forced into a generic time slot.

  • Run the day

    Manifests, digital waivers and check-in are designed to work on a moving deck, in bright sun, with one free hand. Crew can see who is booked, who has signed and who is aboard.

  • Every dollar explained

    A daily close is built to reconcile bookings, payments and payouts to a balanced day. Exports to Xero and MYOB mean your bookkeeper starts from numbers that already agree.

  • Measurable from the first booking

    One booking reference follows each guest across every channel, from first click to check-in. Marketing attribution and server-side conversion tracking are designed in, so you can see which spend fills seats.

  • AI that helps, not decides

    Owners will be able to ask questions of their own data in plain English. AI never changes prices or confirms bookings on its own.

Needs a person

Built to be trusted

These are the rules the assistant is built around.

  • Answers from your real data. Numbers come from your bookings and payments, never from a guess. Every figure is built to be checked a second time against the underlying records before you see it.
  • Your own data is the source of truth. Advertising platforms can receive booking events so their ads can learn, but reports are built to come from LightningRes’s own record of the booking, not from an advertising pixel. A confirmed booking is built to outrank a pixel.
  • You approve every change. It can suggest a sale or draft a message. Nothing is built to change a price or contact a guest until you approve it.
  • It never touches money or capacity. The assistant is built with no ability to take payments, issue refunds, confirm or cancel bookings, or change seats on a departure.
  • It tells you what it can do. Every suggestion is built to say which kind it is, in plain words: “I can do this now”, “I need you to approve this”, or “This needs a person”.
  • Private to your business, and every question is logged. It is built to see only your company’s data, and only to read it. If a question is out of scope, it says so plainly.
  • Tracking is your choice. Connecting advertising accounts is a step you can skip, and nothing about taking bookings depends on it. For operators serving Australia only, advertising consent is off until you turn it on.
  • The booking system never depends on AI. If AI were switched off, bookings, payments and check-in are built to carry on exactly as before.

Your guests pay you, directly

We take nothing from your payments and add nothing to your customer’s price.

  • Each operator connects its own Stripe account through Stripe Connect.
  • Guests pay you directly. You are the merchant of record for your tours.
  • LightningRes is never the merchant of record and never holds guest money.
  • You pay Stripe’s published processing rate, to Stripe. Stripe keeps its processing fee on refunded payments.
  • Your LightningRes subscription is billed separately, based on passengers carried. See pricing.

  • Who counts

    A passenger counts if their booking was confirmed at departure time, from any channel. Crew never count.

  • We don’t charge for cancellations

    Passengers on departures you cancel (weather, breakdown, minimum not reached) and passengers who cancel before departure do not count.

  • 90 days free

    No card, no cap.

  • Billed monthly, in arrears

    You pay for passengers actually carried. We tell you the amount three days before your card is charged and send a receipt. No annual contract and no minimum term.

  • Leave any time

    Take a full data export whenever you like. If you stop paying, your account goes dormant: read-only access and full export for 12 months, nothing deleted, one-click reactivation.

  • No surprise price rises

    Prices change only on 30 days’ written notice, from the next billing period. Cancel before then and you pay nothing further.
